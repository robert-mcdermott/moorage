Onboarding

Your first week, in order.

Day one

Pick up your laptop, sign in to the team's accounts, and join the team's chat. Your onboarding buddy walks you through the rest of the day.

The first week

Read the deploying guide, ship a small change to one of the apps, and pair with someone on a bigger one.

Who to ask

Your buddy first, then anyone on the team: asking is how the handbook gets better.
